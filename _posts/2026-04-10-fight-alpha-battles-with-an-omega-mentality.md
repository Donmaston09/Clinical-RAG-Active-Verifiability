# Fight Alpha Battles with an Omega Mentality
**By Dr. Anthony Onoja**

![Fight Alpha Battles with an Omega Mentality](https://donmaston09.github.io/CraftChronicles/assets/fight-alpha-battles-with-an-omega-mentality.png)

“But they that wait upon the LORD shall renew their strength; they shall mount up with wings as eagles; they shall run, and not be weary; and they shall walk, and not faint.” — Isaiah 40:31

One of the greatest mysteries I have discovered in recent times is that you are not called to fight your enemies merely from a place of knowledge, but from a place of established victory. There is a dimension of warfare that many believers have not yet entered, a realm where you do not struggle to win, but where you fight because the victory has already been secured. By the time you finish reading this, you will understand what it truly means to fight your Alpha battles from already concluded Omega victories.

We are living in perilous times, a season marked by turbulence and uncertainty. We hear of wars and rumours of wars across nations such as Ukraine, Israel, the United States, and Iran. The world grows dimmer by the day, and peace appears to elude humanity. Yet what many fail to understand is that what manifests in the physical is only a reflection of what has already taken shape in the spiritual. Nothing begins here. Everything begins there. The physical world is merely an echo, a stage upon which unseen decisions are revealed. The events we witness on the global stage are not random occurrences. They are manifestations of deeper realities rooted in the unseen realm.

You may ask how and why this is so. The physical world is unstable and volatile, governed by time and chance. It responds to signals, which is why information spreads rapidly through systems such as social media. Yet beyond this realm lies the spiritual, a domain of clarity, balance, and complete knowledge. In that realm, outcomes are not guessed. They are known. Probability does not exist there in the way it does here. What determines results in the physical is who holds authority in the spiritual. The two realms are interconnected. The physical is the womb through which the intentions of the spiritual are manifested. What is conceived in the unseen is eventually delivered in the seen.

What does this mean for you? It means your life did not begin at birth. Long before you entered this world, God had a need, a specific assignment that required your existence. In Isaiah, the Lord asked, “Who shall go for us?” That question still echoes today. God is not asking because He lacks knowledge. He is asking because He requires your agreement. Destiny is not forced upon you. It is entered into by alignment. Yet you cannot commit to what you do not understand. You cannot walk a path you have no awareness of. This is the tragedy of many lives. People move without direction, imitate without understanding, and pursue paths that were never assigned to them. They borrow the maps of others and wonder why they arrive at the wrong destinations.

Human desire complicates this further. Desire, when misaligned, opens the door to dangerous knowledge, the same knowledge that altered the course of humanity in Eden. Knowledge in itself is not evil, but its source and application determine its outcome. When pursued in alignment with God, it reveals identity and clarifies purpose. When pursued through corrupted desire, it empowers darkness and distorts destiny.

Your battles are not random. They are tailored. They are shaped by inherited patterns, personal weaknesses, and spiritual influences that have gained access over time. Most of what people fight stems from these combinations. Yet in the midst of this, Scripture declares that you are made a king and a priest unto God. This is not symbolic language. It is a description of your identity. A king governs, decides, and protects. He is not careless with words or actions. He carries himself with discipline because he understands that his authority affects an entire domain. A priest, on the other hand, stands in the gap. He speaks on behalf of God, carries divine wisdom, intercedes, and watches over spiritual territories. This is who you are meant to be.

When this truth settles within you, your approach to battle changes completely. You no longer fight from confusion. You fight with awareness. You fight with structure. You fight with identity.

First, you must fight with the mentality of an eagle. The eagle sees from a distance. It does not rush blindly into conflict. It selects its target with precision. Not every battle deserves your attention. A king does not fight every noise. He engages only when his kingdom is threatened. You must learn to discern which battles are yours and which are distractions designed to drain your strength.

Secondly, you must fight with vision. The eagle knows exactly what it is pursuing. It does not strike aimlessly. In the same way, you must be deliberate. Do not fight like one beating the air. Fight with clarity and intention. A wise king preserves the resources of his kingdom. He does not engage in unnecessary conflicts that weaken his position. You must avoid decisions that expose you to spiritual vulnerability. Discipline is not restriction. It is protection.

Then comes the deeper revelation. If you watch a film you have already seen, you do not panic at the intense moments because you know how it ends. This is the mentality God calls you to embrace. You are to fight from Omega, the realm of completion, the place where victory is already established. When you confront opposition from this awareness, fear loses its grip. The enemy loses his advantage. You are no longer reacting. You are enforcing what has already been decided. When you know who you are in God, the battle shifts before it even begins.

This understanding transforms your prayer life. Prayer is no longer a desperate request. It becomes a declaration of alignment and a posture of thanksgiving. You approach God not merely as one seeking help, but as a son who understands his inheritance. Scripture calls you a joint heir with Christ. This means you are not fighting for victory. You are walking in it. Even when circumstances suggest otherwise, you must refuse to interpret your reality through fear. Stand firm in faith. Believe that what has been established in the spiritual will manifest in the physical.

Do not be shaken by present conditions. Do not be distracted by delay. Do not envy the paths of others. Fix your gaze on the One who has already written the conclusion of your story. Move with boldness. Advance with conviction. Refuse to shrink.

I pray for you in this season that your mind will be liberated from every limitation that has kept you bound to small thinking. May you rise into the consciousness of victory. May you step into the realm of Omega even as you walk through Alpha. And may you confront every battle before you with the confidence of one who already knows the end.

In Jesus’ name. Amen.

Shalom.
